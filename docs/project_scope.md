# Acquisition scope and unresolved decisions

The user brief in the pasted task is the governing request. The separately supplied `Industrial_Cluster_Location_Proposal.docx.md` was read as research context and left unchanged. It proposes an outcome period of 1992–2020, approximately 1992–2000 predictors, and 10 km baseline with 5 and 25 km sensitivity grids. These are design inputs, not dataset coverage claims.

Acquire only peatland, contemporary port activity, historical-port evidence, coastline/bathymetry, KAPET treatment and separate KEK registry, plus minimal ADM0 reference. Do not fit a model or derive a final suitability index.

Unresolved team decisions: peat cell exclusion threshold and treatment of partial/unknown coverage; choice of historical port continuity assumption; a port accessibility formula; KAPET treatment date among designation, legal effect, incentive availability and operation; policy overlap threshold; whether current coast/bathymetry can support any historical natural-geography claim. Dates observed after treatment cannot be made pre-treatment by the nominal 1992–2000 window.

`EPSG:6933` is the planned equal-area grid CRS. Exchange vectors use EPSG:4326; native rasters remain native. Contemporary and static layers must retain their time limitations.
